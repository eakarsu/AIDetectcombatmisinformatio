# Operations and safety boundary

`start.sh` only starts already-installed services and refuses occupied ports. Bootstrap, migrations, and guarded demo seed are separate scripts under `scripts/`.

`/api/evidence-cases` preserves hashes, custody events, versioned detector runs, analyst transitions, and redress. Scores cannot publish a definitive accusation. Secure object storage, provenance standards, detector workers, fact-check sources, and case-system adapters remain disabled until contracts, credentials, retention review, benchmarks, and integration tests are available.
